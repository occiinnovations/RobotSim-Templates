# Webots

## What is Webots?

Webots is a robot simulator that excels in making robotics education and research quick and easy. It is  powered by a modified Open Dynamics Engine (ODE), allowing users to import, export, program, modify, and simulate complex systems such as industrial arms, drones, humanoids, and autonomous cars. Sensors such as LiDAR and cameras are native as well.

## Why Did I Choose Webots?

I chose to utilize Webots for this project due to its accessibility. Personally, I found the learning curve to be very lenient relative to other industry software. Furthermore, for my purposes it is free, and it is not too compute-heavy.

Undeniably, however, Webots' design makes it straightforward to compare syntax across languages. Since the software includes native support for C++, C, Java, Python out of the box, it may or may not have driven me to build this repo.
