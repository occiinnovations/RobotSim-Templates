# Universal Robots

## What Is Universal Robots?

Univeral Robots (UR) is a robotics company that creates industrial "cobots". It was founded in Denmark in 2005, and unlike many companies, has a bot design allows for humans to work alongside their flagships without intimidation. 

Their flagship arm, the e-Series, is well known and is often used as a default when it comes to training simulations, robotic controllers, and advanced kinematic math. It has 6-DOF, and its actuators are easily identified by the light blue covers. The URe comes in different sizes. For instance, UR10e, UR5e, and UR3e.

## Uses For e-Series

Like practically every 6-DOF arm, there is hardly a limit to the amount of things you can do with an e-Series cobot. However, the friendlier look and simple design open more doors for these models. They have excellent protection features that allow it to lower its hazard level to humans. Their overall slimmer proportions allows addition to classrooms, lab utility counters, and repair shops. 

The software is far more accessible than most market alternatives. PolyScope X allows for control with less abstract programming literacy. Using tablet form, one can use a modern GUI to control and interface with their cobots. This includes moving from x to y, else-ifs, call functions, and TCP. Their cobots are also compatible with industry tools, including ROS and ROS2. It is also very easy to find a URDF file for the e-Series cobot on the internet.
