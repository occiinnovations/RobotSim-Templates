# Universal Robots

## What Is Universal Robots?

Univeral Robots (UR) is a robotics company that creates industrial "cobots". It was founded in Denmark in 2005, and unlike many companies, has a bot design allows for humans to work alongside their flagships without intimidation. 

Their flagship arm, the URe, is well known and is often used as a default when it comes to training simulations, robotic controllers, and advanced kinematic math. It has 6-DOF, and its actuators are easily identified by the light blue covers. The URe comes in different sizes. For instance, UR10e, UR5e, and UR3e.
